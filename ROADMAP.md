# Aqua Nexus — Public Roadmap

This roadmap describes product direction at a high level. Internal
implementation details and proprietary logic are intentionally excluded.

## Current priorities

- Reliability and regression closure
- Mobile and PWA refinement
- Arabic/English localization quality
- Maintenance workflows and printable checklists
- Reporting and alert follow-up
- Long-term history consistency
- Lighting Intelligence refinement
- Backup and recovery hardening

## Next-stage product goals

- Stronger cross-domain Tank Brain event coverage
- Better outcome learning from historical aquarium events
- Improved failure/recovery testing
- Lifecycle flows for vacation, move, restart, and archive
- Richer reporting and historical analysis
- Continued UX polish without weakening operational safety

## Longer-term direction

Aqua Nexus is intended to evolve from a collection of aquarium tools into a
connected operational platform where meaningful events across chemistry,
livestock, equipment, maintenance, lighting, and history inform the user's next
action.

Roadmap items may change as testing and product validation continue.
